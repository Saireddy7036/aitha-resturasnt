Our Story

Passion on Every Plate

Founded by Deepu in 2026, we began as a small family kitchen serving community events. Today, we are one of the region's most trusted catering companies — delivering events across weddings, corporate functions, and private celebrations.

2026

The Beginning

Started as a small family kitchen serving local community celebrations in Birmingham.

2026

Going Professional

Opened our first commercial kitchen, expanded to corporate events and achieved 5-star hygiene rating.

2026

Our Vision

Building a full brigade of culinary professionals to serve weddings, corporates and celebrations across the UK.

Today

1,200+ Events

Trusted by thousands of families, brands, and organisations for unforgettable culinary experiences.

A Team of 40+ Culinary Professionals

Our brigade brings together expertise across Indian, Continental, Mediterranean, and fusion cuisines — ensuring every event reflects the host's personality and the guests' delight. From the first consultation to the final dessert, we handle every detail with care.

We don't just cater events — we craft memories. Whether it's an intimate dinner for 20 or a grand wedding for 600, every plate carries the warmth of a family kitchen and the precision of a professional brigade.
